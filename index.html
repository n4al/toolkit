Research / 03

Relinking Lab

A separate modelling project investigating the production and reproduction of capacities through changing economic relationships.

EXPERIMENTAL RESEARCH DESIGN · NO EMPIRICAL RESULTS YET

Research question

How does changing a specific economic relationship affect the abilities available to particular actors, their control over those abilities and the conditions for reproducing them? What further changes might become possible, and which dependencies remain?

First proposed case: Sabah palm oil

Begin with a bounded producer–processor–machinery supplier relationship, potentially including buyers, financiers and technical institutions. Investigate a baseline machinery purchase against a hypothetical arrangement that adds training, maintenance or research cooperation.

01 / ObserveDocument actors, relationships and evidence.
02 / SpecifyDefine a capability as an ability to perform a particular function.
03 / VaryIntroduce a clearly labelled hypothetical relationship.
04 / TestIdentify prerequisites, remaining dependencies and evidence needed.

How capability would be assessed

DimensionResearch question
ExistsIs the relevant expertise, equipment or organisation present?
AccessibleCan the focal actor actually use it?
ControlledWho can authorise, limit or withdraw access?
CoordinatedCan the necessary actors and resources work together?
ReproducibleCan the function be sustained, funded and passed on?
GenerativeHas a subsequent capability actually been demonstrated?
A proposed relationship is not evidence that capacity has increased. This lab will not assign arbitrary overall capacity scores or treat a scenario as a forecast.

Proposed first prototype

A small illustrative network with approximately ten actors and twenty relationships, an evidence register, baseline and scenario views, and exportable records. All illustrative data will be marked as such. An empirical version will require verified case-specific evidence.

Separate from unequal-exchange calculations

This subnational relational analysis does not compute a “Sabah–China Köhler unequal exchange” figure by treating Sabah as a sovereign currency area. Any national unequal-exchange calculator would be an independently validated module.
