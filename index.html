01 / The toolkit

Different tools.
Your questions.

The toolkit brings together different instruments for explaining, investigating and exploring economic relationships. Use them independently or combine them to pursue your own questions, test ideas and build new research tools.

Each instrument has its own purpose, assumptions and limitations. The toolkit makes these explicit so that different approaches can be connected without treating their findings as interchangeable.

The guiding question

What moves through the world economy?

Who controls the terms of economic relationships, and what does changing those relationships leave actors capable of doing next?

The toolkit investigates existing structures of exchange and dependency alongside the conditions under which alternative relationships might produce and reproduce new capacities.
