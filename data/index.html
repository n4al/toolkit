Data Catalogue

Finding, documenting and evaluating data for the study of unequal exchange, dependency and global production.

This catalogue identifies potential sources. Listing a dataset does not mean that it has been downloaded, validated or incorporated into the toolkit.

Research standards

What a dataset entry should record

Each future catalogue entry should document the provider, source URL, variables, units, geographical coverage, time period, classifications, licence, access conditions, known limitations and date accessed.

Country-level statistics must not automatically be presented as subnational evidence. For example, Malaysian national data cannot be assumed to describe Sabah without further justification.

Where datasets are combined, transformations and compatibility decisions should be documented and reproducible.
