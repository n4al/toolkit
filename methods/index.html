Methods Library

Unequal exchange is investigated through different theoretical frameworks, methods and units of analysis.

This library will document those approaches without treating their measurements as interchangeable.

Purpose

What are we measuring?

Different approaches to unequal exchange investigate different relationships: international price formation, labour embodied in trade, material and energy flows, and the distribution of value across production networks.

Each method requires its own definitions, data, assumptions and interpretation. A result produced using one approach cannot automatically be substituted for a result produced using another.

Research approaches

Areas for documentation

Method family

Monetary approaches

Approaches examining international prices, wages, productivity and transfers of value.

Future documentation will distinguish specific models and their assumptions.

Method family

Embodied labour

Approaches tracing labour embodied in internationally traded goods and services.

Future documentation will identify the input-output data, labour measures and valuation choices used.

Method family

Materials and energy

Approaches examining the physical resources embodied in trade, including material and energy flows.

Physical quantities and monetary valuations will be distinguished.

Related analysis

Global production networks

Approaches investigating ownership, production, value capture and control across interconnected economic activities.

These analyses may complement unequal-exchange research but should not be presented as equivalent measurements.

Documentation standard

What each method entry should contain

As the library develops, individual entries should specify the research question, theoretical basis, unit of analysis, required datasets, calculation procedure, assumptions, geographical coverage and limitations.

Published methods and any independently implemented calculations will be clearly distinguished. No calculator is presented here as validated or operational.

Project status

This page establishes the structure of the Methods Library. Detailed methodological entries, citations and reproducible implementations will be added as they are reviewed.
