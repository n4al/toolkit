How to read this page

Documentation is not replication. A documented method has been explained; a reconstructed analysis has been independently implemented to a stated extent. Neither status implies endorsement of the original study or verification of every result.

Research instruments are identified separately: they are tools for inquiry, not replications of published findings. Consult each entry for its evidence and limitations.
