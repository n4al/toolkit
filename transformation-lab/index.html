Research instruments and experiments

Transformation Lab

How do economic transformations become possible, and what determines their limits?

The Transformation Lab provides research instruments for investigating the conditions, mechanisms and limits of economic transformation.

Its tools help researchers examine existing arrangements, interrogate assumptions, specify proposed interventions and identify the evidence needed to investigate change.

Relinking is one strategy explored within the Lab, not a predetermined outcome. Instruments may be used independently or combined in a research project.

How to use the Lab

Browse the instruments below. Each provides a different way of formulating research questions and identifying what would need to be established empirically.

Experimental tools are not evidence of successful transformation. Their assumptions, applications and limitations should be examined separately.

Audit and diagnosis

Model & Capacity Audit

Examine the capacities assumed, exercised, produced or reproduced in a published model, historical case or proposed intervention.

Research focus: Assumptions, capacity formation, control, reproduction and evidence gaps.

Worked application: Samir Amin (1987), A Note on the Concept of Delinking.

Scenario exploration

Relinking Scenario Builder

Explore how a proposed change in economic relationships might enable particular functions and capacities to develop.

Research focus: Functions, knowledge access, control, dependencies and continuity.

Illustrative application: A hypothetical maintenance and training arrangement involving a Sabah palm-oil processor.

Status: Conceptual prototype; no empirical results.

03 / Development

An evolving research space

The Lab currently contains two experimental instruments. Additional tools and applications can be developed as research questions and evidence requirements become clearer.

A research instrument, a worked application and an empirical result are distinct contributions. Future development will preserve those distinctions.
