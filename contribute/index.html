Research integrity

Principles for contributions

Contributions should identify their sources, explain their assumptions and distinguish established findings from interpretation, proposed methods and hypothetical scenarios.

Different approaches to unequal exchange must not be combined without a documented justification. Data should retain its geographical and temporal scope: national statistics cannot automatically stand in for subnational evidence.

Proposed calculations should be reproducible and should not be presented as validated until appropriate checks have been completed.

Do not upload confidential, personally identifiable or otherwise restricted research material to the public repository. Check permissions and licensing before sharing third-party data.
